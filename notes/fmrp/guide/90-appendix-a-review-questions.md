# Appendix A — Review Questions

Candidates should note that the **sole purpose of the Review Questions is to familiarise candidates with the scope and general nature of the examinations, and the format of the examination questions**. They are **not intended as preparatory study material**, and **do not cover all the material tested**. The correct answer for each question is in **bold** in the source booklet and is reproduced as **Answer** below. Where a question offers four statements (a–d) followed by combinations, the correct **combination** is given.

## Chapter 1 — Introduction

**1.** Which of the following asset classes / products are covered in the Blue Book?

- **a.** Equity securities
- **b.** Non-deliverable forwards (NDFs)
- **c.** Money market instruments
- **d.** OTC derivatives

Options: (a) and (b) · (b) and (c) · **(b), (c) and (d)** · (a), (b) and (c)

**Answer: (b), (c) and (d).**

**2.** Which of the following statements relating to Capital Markets Services (CMS) licences is CORRECT?

- **a.** A CMS licence holder must either be a Bank or a Merchant Bank and cannot be other types of financial institutions.
- **b.** **Merchant Banks can carry out any regulated activities under the Securities and Futures Act (SFA) without being separately licensed as a CMS licence holder under the SFA.**
- **c.** Merchant Banks can ignore the business conduct requirements prescribed in the SFA in respect of the regulated activities under the SFA.
- **d.** The MAS must grant a CMS licence to merchant banks in order for them to conduct any regulated activities under the SFA.

**Answer: b.**

**4.** Which of the following statements relating to dealing for personal accounts is TRUE?

- **b.** Representatives cannot execute any personal trade on the same securities placed by customers.
- **c.** **Representatives must avoid any conflicts of interest with their professional roles while dealing for personal accounts.**
- **d.** Representatives should strictly not be allowed to deal for personal accounts.

**5.** Which of the following are considered "persons deemed connected to a dealing staff member"?

- **d.** Step-children of the staff member.

**6.** Under which circumstance would a Market Participant be required to submit a "Misconduct Report" to MAS?

- **c.** The Representative has done trades with a counterparty with a low credit rating.

**Answer: b.**

**7.** Which of the following statements most correctly describes the appropriate action a Market Participant should take when it receives money from or on behalf of a customer?

- **c.** The Market Participant cannot use the money at all.
- **d.** **The Market Participant must use the money for any purpose as instructed by the customer.**

**Answer: d.** — Customer money is applied **as the customer directs**; internal approval does not widen the permitted use.

**8.** Under what situation must a Market Participant provide its customer with a separate written risk disclosure document as prescribed under the Securities and Futures Act?

## Chapter 3 — Confidentiality and Information Sharing

**9.** Which of the following statements is TRUE about confidentiality and customer anonymity principles?

- **a.** **Customer data should be disclosed in accordance with the applicable laws and regulations.**
- **b.** Customer verification can only be done by checking with the customer directly.
- **c.** The customer must complete the account opening form in front of the employee in the bank branch.
- **d.** The responsibility for maintaining confidentiality of information rests with the customer.

**Answer: a.** — Confidentiality rests with the **Market Participant**, not the customer, and verification may be done through independent sources.

**10.** Which of the following would be considered confidential information in relation to financial markets?

- **a.** Customer or counterparty name, and any other information that may lead to the identification of the customer or counterparty.
- **b.** Details of a Market Participant's order book.
- **c.** Spread matrices provided by Market Participants to their clients.
- **d.** Spreads on bids and offers available in the market.

Options: **(a), (b) and (c)** · (b) and (c) · (b), (c) and (d) · (c) and (d)

**Answer: (a), (b) and (c).**

**11.** Market Participants only disclose confidential information to internal and/or external parties under specific circumstances, such as:

- **a.** When required under the Income Tax Act (Cap 134).
- **b.** When required under the Banking Act, Section 47 — Banking Secrecy, and Parts I and II of the Third Schedule.
- **c.** To investigate or prosecute an offence that is alleged or suspected to have been committed.
- **d.** When required by other Market Participants.

Options: **(a), (b) and (c)** · (b) and (c) · (b), (c) and (d) · (c) and (d)

**Answer: (a), (b) and (c).** — A request from another Market Participant is **not** a lawful basis for disclosure.

**12.** Market Participants should communicate in a manner that is clear, accurate, professional and not misleading. To ensure this, Market Participants should do which of the following?

- **a.** Not communicate false information.
- **b.** Not provide misleading information in order to protect confidential information (i.e. when executing partial orders).
- **c.** Decline to disclose whether their request to transact is in the full quantity of the order or not.
- **d.** Not identify opinions as opinions.

Options: (a), (b) and (c) · (b) and (c) · **(a) and (b)** · (c) and (d)

**Answer: (a) and (b).**

**13.** Market Participants should communicate market colour appropriately and without compromising confidential information. Which of the following are appropriate ways to share market colour?

- **a.** Bank Salesperson to hedge fund: "We've seen large NZD/USD demand from Real Money names this morning."
- **b.** Asset manager to bank market maker: "Can you give me some colour around the 100 point rally in GBP/USD in the past hour?"
- **c.** Bank salesperson to hedge fund: "We've seen large NZD/USD demand from Royal Bank of Singapore this morning."
- **d.** Market maker to hedge fund: "Yen liquidity has deteriorated. Just now it took me 15 ticks to cover my sale of 100 million USD/JPY to a Japanese automaker."

Options: (a) and (d) · (b) and (c) · **(a) and (b)** · (c) and (d)

**Answer: (a) and (b).** — Naming a specific counterparty (c) or describing a specific client's trade in identifiable detail (d) breaches confidentiality.

## Chapter 4 — Governance, Risk Management and Compliance

**14.** Which one of the following statements relating to segregation of duties is TRUE?

- **a.** Back office staff should not have any communication with front office personnel.
- **b.** It is acceptable for front office, middle office and back office to share access to their respective systems.
- **c.** **Management should establish appropriate information barriers between trading, risk management and processing units.**
- **d.** Market Participants should not share any trading profit and loss reports with the middle office.

**Answer: c.**

**15.** What would be an effective internal control procedure to mitigate the risk of rogue traders committing fraud?

- **a.** Front office personnel cannot communicate with staff in the processing unit to resolve issues.
- **b.** Front office personnel should not contact middle office staff to resolve trade related issues.
- **c.** **Front office personnel should not have access rights to middle office systems to process trades.**
- **d.** Front office personnel should not physically be at the back office department area.

**Answer: c.** — The control is **system access**, not a ban on communication.

**16.** Which of the following constitute anti-money laundering (AML) and know-your-customer (KYC) requirements that are relevant to Market Participants?

- **a.** Establish internal procedures and controls to mitigate AML and KYC risk.
- **b.** Establish procedures to inform customers when a report has been made to the authorities for suspicious transactions.
- **c.** Maintain records of all CDD documentation.
- **d.** Perform customer due diligence (CDD) for all customers.

Options: (a) and (b) · (c) and (d) · (a) and (c) · **(a), (c) and (d)**

**Answer: (a), (c) and (d).** — **Tipping off** a customer that a suspicious transaction report has been made is itself an offence.

**17.** Which of the following is an example of operational risk?

- **a.** Risk of losses as a result of an adverse movement in the markets.
- **b.** Risk of losses due to holding a short position in a particular financial instrument.
- **c.** **Risk as a result of inadequate processes and controls.**
- **d.** Risk caused by a counterparty going bankrupt.

**Answer: c.**

**18.** Which one of the following actions should be taken to minimise losses when a customer fails to meet margin top-up requirements?

- **a.** **Close out the customer's positions if margin calls remain unanswered.**
- **b.** Contact the customer to request settlement instructions.
- **c.** Execute a trade for the customer that hedges against the loss-making position.
- **d.** Issue a contract note on the trade done.

**Answer: a.**

**19.** Which of the following is a key control that should be implemented for off-premises dealing?

- **a.** Trade details of "off-premises" trades should be clearly documented on the letterhead of the Market Participant.
- **b.** Trade details of "off-premises" trades should be clearly reported to the Compliance Department.
- **c.** Trade details of "off-premises" trades should be clearly reported to the Risk Department.
- **d.** **Trade details of "off-premises" trades should promptly be reported to the counterparty via approved communication channels.**

**Answer: d.**

**20.** In business continuity planning, which of the following is MOST important?

- **a.** A contingency plan for front office Representatives to ensure that they can continue dealing in any market disruption event.
- **b.** **A contingency plan for treasury operations in the front, middle and back offices to ensure that these operations can continue in any market disruption event.**
- **c.** A contingency plan for Representatives to work from home to continue performing treasury operations in the front, middle and back office areas in any market disruption event.
- **d.** Internal Standing Instructions to withhold all payments during any market disruption event.

**Answer: b.** — Coverage must span **front, middle and back office**; withholding all payments (d) contradicts the prohibition on unilateral suspension.

## Chapter 5 — Execution and Handling of Orders

**24.** What should be done by an IDB when it receives an order from a Principal with special conditions?

- **a.** **IDBs must accurately relay the special conditions to the counterparties.**
- **b.** IDBs should advise the Principal not to impose the special conditions.
- **c.** IDBs should exclude the special conditions.
- **d.** IDBs should increase their brokerage for orders with special conditions.

**Answer: a.**

**25.** Which of the following actions should NOT be done by IDBs?

- **a.** **Engage in taking its own positions by executing its own trades.**
- **b.** Provide settlement instructions to the counterparties.
- **c.** Provide the names of the counterparties after the deal has been concluded.
- **d.** Relay price quotations upon receipt of an order from a counterparty.

**Answer: a.** — An IDB acts as an intermediary and must not take **principal** positions.

**26.** If a price maker's price is dealt simultaneously by two or more IDBs, what must the price maker do?

- **a.** Choose which IDB to honour his quoted amount.
- **b.** Honour, subject to limit, his quoted amount, split equally with each IDB.
- **c.** Honour, subject to limit, his quoted amount, with each IDB.
- **d.** **Honour, subject to limit, the minimum dealing amount with each IDB.**

**Answer: d.**

**27.** What types of transactions are restricted under MAS's regulations on Lending of Singapore Dollar to Non-Resident Financial Institutions?

- **a.** A foreign bank in Singapore executes a EUR/SGD FX swap where the first leg is to buy SGD 10 million from its Head Office in Europe.
- **b.** A foreign bank in Singapore executes a EUR/SGD FX swap where the first leg is to sell SGD 10 million to its Head Office in Europe.
- **c.** A foreign bank in Singapore lends SGD 6 million to a finance company licensed in Singapore.
- **d.** A foreign bank in Singapore lends SGD 6 million to its head office in Europe.

Options: (a) and (d) · **(b) and (d)** · (a) and (c) · (b) and (c)

**Answer: (b) and (d).** — The restriction targets **SGD lending to non-resident institutions**, including via a swap leg that sells SGD offshore.

**28.** Which of the following is TRUE about "stop-loss" orders?

- **a.** **There is no guarantee of fixed price execution to the counterparty.**
- **b.** The trade should be immediately concluded at the highest market price with the counterparty.
- **c.** The trade should be immediately concluded at the lowest market price with the counterparty.
- **d.** Trade will be executed at a fixed price with the counterparty.

**Answer: a.** — A stop-loss converts to a **market order** once triggered, so slippage is possible.

**29.** What is the appropriate practice for trade cancellations?

- **a.** Trades can be cancelled before the settlement date.
- **b.** Trades must always be cancelled if the price traded is not representative of the prevailing market price.
- **c.** **Trades will only be subject to cancellation where both parties to the trade agree to the cancellation.**
- **d.** Trades with minor errors in their notional value should be subject to cancellation.

**Answer: c.**

**30.** What are the market trading hours for foreign exchange transactions?

- **a.** **0500h Sydney time on a Monday morning to 1700h New York time on a Friday.**
- **b.** 0900h to 1700h daily.
- **c.** 0900h to 1700h from Monday to Friday.
- **d.** 0900h Sydney time on a Monday morning to 1700h New York time on a Friday.

**Answer: a.** — Note **0500h** Sydney, not 0900h: the FX market runs continuously from the Sydney open to the New York close.

**31.** Which of the following should be observed when trading FX swaps?

- **a.** The forward rate of the far leg of the FX swap should be determined on the value date of the near leg.
- **b.** The near leg of the FX swap must always be value spot.
- **c.** The price maker has the right to set the spot rate in an FX swap.
- **d.** **The setting of the spot rate in an FX swap transaction should be done immediately after a price has been hit and before clearing of the counterparty's name.**

**32.** Jane is an FX dealer. What should Jane do before transacting an off-market rate FX trade with a corporate customer?

- **b.** Obtain the express approval of the customer's senior management.

**33.** A Market Participant does not have sufficient limits to accommodate the counterparty's full amount but is trying to raise limits. What action should be taken?

- **d.** Inform the IDB that it does not have sufficient limits.

**34.** What should a Market Participant do before it enters into a trade as principal with a customer in respect of securities traded on a local or overseas securities exchange?

## How to use these questions

The set **familiarises candidates with scope, general nature and format**; it does not cover all examination material and is not a substitute for the chapters. Several answers turn on a single qualifying word, and those are worth studying first:

- **Q7** turns on *whose* instruction governs the use of customer money — the **customer's**, not an internal approver's.
- **Q9** reverses the party who bears confidentiality: it rests with the **Market Participant**.
- **Q11** rules out disclosure merely because **another Market Participant requests it**.
- **Q13** separates acceptable **market colour** (aggregated or anonymous) from naming a counterparty or describing a client's trade in identifiable terms.
- **Q15** is about **system access rights**, not a prohibition on communication between desks.
- **Q16** excludes **tipping off** — informing a customer that a suspicious transaction report has been made is itself an offence.
- **Q20** requires **front, middle and back office** coverage, and rejects withholding all payments, which contradicts the no-unilateral-suspension principle.
- **Q25** and **Q26** define the IDB role: an IDB does not take principal positions, and a price maker dealt simultaneously must honour the **minimum dealing amount** with each IDB subject to limit.
- **Q28** turns on the difference between a stop-loss and a **fixed-price** execution — there is none; there is no price guarantee.
- **Q30** is the one to memorise precisely: **0500h Sydney Monday to 1700h New York Friday**.
- **Q33** rewards the answer that states what **can** be done rather than simply declining.
- **Q37** assumes **no simultaneous delta hedge**.
- **Q40** restricts close-out netting to transactions with the **defaulting counterparty**.
- **Q41** distinguishes the **core documents** (Master Agreement and Schedule) from the **Credit Support Annex** and confirmation templates.
- **Q43** prefers **effective** communication to **non-stop** communication.
- **Q44** is MAS-specific for **SGD** settlement.

The expanded question bank in `notes/fmrp/FMRP_QUESTIONS.md` covers these themes and adds application questions beyond the orientation set.
