# FMRP MCQ Revision Bank

This bank contains 300 one-best-answer questions: 48 source-reference questions adapted from Appendix A of the supplied study guide and 252 original questions based on the teaching guide. Every question has four choices. Answers and rationales are inside collapsed disclosure blocks in HTML; the printable PDF is question-only.

Source-reference questions retain the source's statement-combination style where used. The source questions are reproduced for scope and format practice and should not be treated as a complete syllabus.

## Chapter 4 - Governance, Risk Management and Compliance

### 14. **[Source reference | Single choice]** [FMRP §4.4] Which one of the following statements relating to segregation of duties is TRUE?

- b. It is acceptable for front office, middle office and back office to share access to their respective systems.
- c. Management should establish appropriate information barriers between trading, risk management and processing units.

<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** C. Management should establish appropriate information barriers between trading, risk management and processing units.
