# FMRP MCQ Revision Bank

This bank contains 300 one-best-answer questions: 48 source-reference questions adapted from Appendix A of the supplied study guide and 252 original questions based on the teaching guide. Every question has four choices. Answers and rationales are inside collapsed disclosure blocks in HTML; the printable PDF is question-only.

Source-reference questions retain the source's statement-combination style where used. The source questions are reproduced for scope and format practice and should not be treated as a complete syllabus.

## Chapter 4 - Governance, Risk Management and Compliance

### 14. **[Source reference | Single choice]** [FMRP §4.4] Which one of the following statements relating to segregation of duties is TRUE?

<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** C. Management should establish appropriate information barriers between trading, risk management and processing units.

- a. To advise the counterparty of the full economic details of the trade
- b. To detect incorrect economic details before settlement
- c. To reduce operational risk
- d. To obtain senior management approval of the trade

<details>
<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** A, B, C

**Rationale:** Confirmation is a control on accuracy and a record of the trade. It is not an approval mechanism; approval happens before execution.

### 371. **[Original | Single choice]** [FMRP §6] A third party payment is requested by a counterparty. A Market Participant may make it:

<summary>Show answer</summary>

**Answer:** A. Where the counterparty instructs it with a valid reason and supporting documentation

**Rationale:** Third party payments are permitted where there is a genuine commercial reason and documentation supports it; the control is evidence, not a blanket prohibition.

### 372. **[Original | Single choice]** [FMRP §6] Under the ISDA Master Agreement, on a counterparty default the non-defaulting party may:

### 376. **[Original | Single choice]** [FMRP §6] A confirmation is sent and the counterparty disputes a material term. The correct response is to:

### 381. **[Original | Single choice]** [FMRP §7] A party considers suspending settlement unilaterally because its own systems have failed. It should:

### 389. **[Original | Single choice]** [FMRP §8] Which parties are involved in the benchmark rate setting process?

### 390. **[Original | Single choice]** [FMRP §8] A dealer handling customer fixing orders in relation to a traded benchmark should:

### 391. **[Original | Single choice]** [FMRP §8] A dealer is responsible for both benchmark contributions and opinions on benchmarks. To manage the conflict he should:
