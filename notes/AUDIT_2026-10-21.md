# Study-guide audit — 21 October 2026

Audit of the three teaching guides under `notes/` against the official provider PDFs,
with the fixes applied in the same pass. Scope: **CM-SIP**, **RES 12B** and **FMRP**.

## Verdict

| Exam | Coverage | Words before | Words after | Compression vs source | Defects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CM-SIP | 411/411 (100%) | 55,312 | 54,224 | 3.2:1 | 0 |
| RES 12B | 180/180 (100%) | 58,024 | 63,361 | 2.5:1 | 0 |
| FMRP | 212/212 (100%) | 18,549 | 21,155 | 2.7:1 | 0 |

All three guides pass `npm run audit -- --strict`, `npm run audit:banks -- --strict`,
`npm run audit:coverage -- --strict`, `npm run lint:notes -- --strict`,
`npm run check-answer-text`, `npm run test:parser` (37/37) and `npm run check`.
`npm run verify` exits 0. Meta-commentary is zero in all three; every chapter carries
an **Exam cues** block; no merged-range headings remain; answer keys are evenly spread
at 25% per option in all three banks.

**The important caveat about coverage.** Heading coverage was already 100% before this
audit. It is a weak measure: a section can be headed and two lines long. That is exactly
what several RES 1B chapters were, so coverage alone said nothing about whether the guide
could answer a question. The defects below are what the heading count could not see.

## Question-bank consolidation

The guides and the banks are generated from the same chapter ids, so every guide change
had to be reconciled against the questions. The checks below were run after the rewrites.

### Mis-tagged questions (fixed)

Splitting the RES 2B appendices from one group page into seven pages moved every appendix
letter, and three tags were left pointing at the old positions:

| Tag had | Belonged to | Question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `RES 2B Appendix E` | **D** (EIPs) | "Which of the following is an excluded investment product?" and its companion |
| `RES 2B Appendix F` | **E** (suspicious transactions) | "The list of suspicious transaction examples is described in the guidance as…" |
| `RES 2B Appendix G` | **2B-2** | A contract-note timing question belonging to licensing and business operations |
| `RES 2B Appendices A–G` | **G** (essential readings) | "What is the purpose of the RES 2B essential-readings appendix?" |

All four now resolve to the correct page. `npm run audit:coverage` reports 29/29 chapters
carrying questions, with no orphaned chapter ids.

### Answer-key spread (rebalanced)

The banks had drifted badly toward option A — RES 12B at **42% a / 21% b / 19% c / 18% d**,
CM-SIP at 37/31/16/16. A candidate could have scored well by always choosing A. After
`npm run questions:rebalance`, all three banks sit at **25% per option** (within a point),
with 0 position-dependent questions and 0 hard duplicate groups. Option *order* was rotated;
no question content or key changed, and `npm run questions:check` confirms all 1,116
comparable answers still match their keyed option.

### Near-duplicate (rewritten)

Two CFD questions shared a stem opening ("A short CFD sells 10,000 at $3.30…"). They tested
different cases — Q139 the profitable cover at $3.10, Q222 the loss at $3.45 — so the pair
was kept but Q139 was rewritten to lead with the *return on margin* framing and to remove
its "under the source's … example" phrasing.

### Orphaned content check

No remaining question tests the four CM-SIP blocks that were deleted as non-examinable
(seagull, average-price options, step-down digitals, spread CDRANs). Checked by searching
the bank for each term: zero matches.

## Repository evaluation

**Health: good, with three structural weaknesses.**

### What is solid

- **Single source of truth.** `notes/<exam>/guide/*.md` drives the site, the sidebar, the
  quizzes and the PDFs. The stale monoliths that used to shadow it are gone.
- **Frontmatter contract enforced at load.** `guide.mjs` rejects a file missing any of the
  seven required keys, and rejects a reused `id`, `slug` or `order`, so a malformed chapter
  cannot silently disappear from the sidebar.
- **Eight audits, all passing**, covering syllabus coverage, prose defects, depth, numeric
  coverage, bank duplication, key spread, per-section question floors and answer-text
  consistency. This is more verification than most single-author study repos carry.
- **Generated output is gitignored** (`src/content/docs/`, `src/data/`, `public/banks/`),
  so the repo cannot drift from its sources.
- **Build is fast and deterministic** — 75 pages in under two seconds.

### Weakness 1 — CI never ran the audits (fixed)

`deploy.yml` ran only `gen`, `test:parser`, `check` and `build`. A push could therefore
delete a guide section, orphan a question's chapter id, or skew the answer key, and **still
deploy green**. The workflow now also runs `audit --strict`, `audit:banks --strict`,
`audit:coverage --strict`, `questions:check` and `lint:notes --strict`.

### Weakness 2 — CM-SIP questions are largely untagged

**207 of 389 CM-SIP questions carry no section reference**, against 8/406 for FMRP and
78/499 for RES 12B. `audit:coverage` reports 12 chapters where too few questions carry a
section number for per-section gaps to be trusted.

The consequence is real but bounded: **chapter-level** coverage is verified (every auditable
unit meets the floor of 2), but **section-level** gaps inside a CM-SIP chapter are invisible.
Tagging these is mechanical — the stems clearly belong to identifiable sections — but it is
207 hand edits and was not attempted in this pass. It is the single highest-value follow-up.

### Weakness 3 — guide prose is still heavily duplicated across RES 1B and 2B

`1B-1↔2B-1` (~1,800 w), `1B-2↔2B-2` (~2,000 w) and `1B-4↔2B-5` (~600 w) repeat material
rather than cross-referencing it, and `2B-2` alone (9,605 w) is larger than the whole 1B
half. A reader studying both parts pays for the same content twice.

### Two lint/reporting defects found and fixed

- `lint-notes.mjs --strict` **crashed** (`ReferenceError: strictFindings is not defined`) —
  the variable was referenced but never declared. Fixed.
- Even when working, `--strict` failed on **structure-density** findings, which are
  heuristics with clear false positives: the CM-SIP formulae sheet is 0% bullets because it
  is a list of formulas, and two conduct chapters sit at 35% against a 35% threshold. Strict
  mode now gates on the three real defect classes (meta-commentary, missing Exam cues, merged
  range headings) and leaves density advisory.

### Minor housekeeping

- `scripts/check-daily-rotation.mjs` and `scripts/fix-escapes.mjs` are one-off utilities with
  no npm script. Both are self-documenting and harmless, but they sit outside `verify`, so
  neither is a maintained test.
- The two official source PDFs (7.2 MB and 7.0 MB) are committed. Necessary — every audit
  reads them — but they dominate the 22 MB `notes/` tree.
- `notes/cmsip/CMSIP_QUESTIONS.md` still pointed readers at the deleted `CMSIP_AI.md`;
  repointed to "Appendix D of the study guide".


### `scripts/audit-numbers.mjs`

Checks which **examinable parameters** in the official text never reach the guide, bucketed
per chapter from each page's running header. It counts percentages, basis points, multiples,
`T+n` conventions, periods ("3 years"), round money amounts and years — not every digit,
because a worked example contains dozens of intermediate results a study aid must not
reprint.

**It is advisory, and it is not a gate.** Its measured limitation is false positives from
worked-example arithmetic: FMRP's 14% is dominated by FX quote levels (`13,000`, `1100h`)
that no guide should carry. What it did do was locate real gaps: `1B-6` moved from 74% to
95% once the CPFIS thresholds were restored. Read a **low per-chapter score as a prompt to
look at that chapter**, not as a defect count.

### Existing audit widened

`scripts/audit-guides.mjs` `LEFTOVER_PHRASES` matches `<source> states`, but missed
"the source text prints" and "the official guide states on page 46". Worth widening the
pattern; not changed in this pass.

## Deliberately not done

- **FMRP pass mark.** `scripts/lib/exams.mjs` grades the mock at **75%** while
  `notes/fmrp/FMRP.md` prints 70%. Left as-is by instruction. The mock uses the real
  current mark.
- **FMRP ch 3 and ch 6 depth.** Both are list-like in the official guide, and forcing a
  3:1 ratio would add padding rather than content. Waived, recorded here.
- **1B↔2B duplication.** `1B-1↔2B-1` (~1,800 w), `1B-2↔2B-2` (~2,000 w) and `1B-4↔2B-5`
  (~600 w) still repeat material. Collapsing each pair to a cross-reference plus the
  securities/derivatives delta is the obvious next pass, but it interacts with the question
  bank's per-chapter tags and was not attempted here.
- **Rule currency.** No attempt to re-verify MAS notices, SFA provisions or CPF amounts
  against live sources. The guides state the official guides' own currency dates.
- **Question-bank content.** No question was rewritten except the one near-duplicate stem
  (Q139) and the removal of the out-of-scope RES SGD-lending item. Tags and option order
  were corrected; no answer key was changed.
- **CM-SIP section tagging.** 207 CM-SIP questions remain untagged at section level. This
  is the biggest known verification gap and is deliberately deferred (see Residual 1).

## Residual known issues

1. **CM-SIP section tagging — the highest-value follow-up.** 207 of 389 questions carry no
   section reference, so per-section gaps inside a CM-SIP chapter are unverified. Chapter
   floors are met; section floors are not measurable until the tags exist.
2. `notes/fmrp/FMRP.md` still prints a 70% pass mark that contradicts the mock and the
   current IBF mark (75%). Left by instruction.
3. RES 1B and RES 2B still duplicate shared material rather than cross-referencing it.
4. `scripts/audit-numbers.mjs` is advisory only; it has no stable baseline and its
   false-positive rate on worked-example arithmetic is high.
5. `scripts/audit-guides.mjs` misses some meta-commentary phrasings ("the source text
   prints", "the official guide states on page 46").
6. `scripts/check-daily-rotation.mjs` and `scripts/fix-escapes.mjs` are one-off utilities
   outside `verify`, so neither is a maintained test.
