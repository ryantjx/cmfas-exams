## Financial Markets Regulatory Practices (FMRP) Examinations

- [FMRP](https://www.ibf.org.sg/home/exams/examinations/fmrp/examination-details/register)
- Examination Details: 100 MCQ, 2 hours, 70% passing mark
- Syllabus
  - Introduction
  - Ethics, Behavioural Standards and Professional Conduct
  - Confidentiality and Information Sharing
  - Governance, Risk Management and Compliance
  - Execution and Handling of Orders
  - Confirmation and Settlement
  - Handling Market Disruptions
  - Benchmark Rate Setting

## CM-SIP - Derivatives and Collective Investment Schemes (SIP)

- [CM-SIP](https://www.cmfas.com.sg/cmfas_cm-sip_exam)
- Exam Details: 80 MCQ, 2.5 hours, 70% passing mark
- Syllabus
    - Overview of Derivatives
    - Futures, Forwards & Swaps
    - Futures Strategies
    - Options
    - Structured Warrants and Daily Leveraged Certificates
    - Barrier Options, Binary Options and Callable Contracts
    - Structured Deposits and Other Structured Products
    - Structured Notes
    - Structured Funds
    - Contracts for Differences (CFDs)
    - Key Product and investment risks for Derivatives and Structured Products
    - Case Studies

## RES 12B - Rules, Ethics and Skills for Securities and Derivatives Dealers of Non-Exchange Memberst

- [RES 12B](https://www.cmfas.com.sg/cmfas_res_12b_exam)
- Exam Details: 60 MCQ, 1.5 hours, 75% passing mark
- Syllabus
  - Res 1B: Rules, Ethics and Skills for Securities and Derivatives Dealers of Non-Exchange Members
    - The Capital Markets Industry in Singapore and Participants in the Capital Markets
    - Licensing and Business Operations
    - Market Conduct
    - Ethics, Codes and Standards of Professional Conduct for Securities Dealing
    - Securities Dealing Practices and Skills
    - CPFIS
    - Prevention of Financial Crimes
  - Res 2B: Rules, Ethics and Skills for Securities and Derivatives Dealers of Non-Exchange Members
    - The Capital Markets Industry in Singapore and Participants in the Capital Markets
    - Licensing and Business Operations
    - Market Conduct
    - Over-the-Counter Derivatives
    - Ethics, Codes and Standards of Professional Conduct for Derivatives Dealing
    - Derivatives Dealing Practices and Skills
    - Prevention of Financial Crimes

## Research (compiled 2026-10-09)

All three exams are **MAS-recognised, administered by IBF**, and are **on-demand computer-based tests** (not fixed annual sittings). They are taken at the **IBF Assessment Centre** — 20 Anson Road, #05-01, Twenty Anson, Singapore 079912. You select a date/time slot when you book through the [IBF Portal](https://eservices.ibf.org.sg).

### Summary comparison

| Exam | Format | Duration | Pass mark | Fee (non-member, incl. GST) | Exemptions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FMRP | 100 MCQ, computer-based | 2 h | **75%** (75/100) | ~$250.70 ($207.10 corporate member) | **None** |
| CM-SIP (ex-M6A) | 80 MCQ incl. 6 case studies (18 Q) | 2.5 h | **70%** (56/80) | ~$250.70 ($207.10 member) | Yes — per MAS Notice SFA 04-N22 |
| RES 12B (= RES 1B + RES 2B) | 60 MCQ, computer-based | 1.5 h | **75%** (45/60) | ~$501.40 ($414.20 corporate member) | **None** |

Notes:

- Fees vary by source and whether GST (9%) is included. CMFAS Academy quotes ex-GST; IBF and ExamPrep.sg quote incl. GST. **Always verify on the IBF portal.**
- RES 12B is a **combined** paper. If you fail any part you fail the whole exam and pay full fees to retake. CMFAS Academy recommends sitting **RES 1B and RES 2B separately**.
- Since 1 April 2024, all IBF exams use 4-option MCQs (answers may be a mix, e.g. "B and C").

### Study period (no official "average" published — provider recommendations used as proxy)

| Exam | Recommended study hours | Realistic calendar (part-time) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FMRP | **88 h** (CMFAS Academy) / ~56 h (ExamPrep.sg) | 3–6 weeks |
| CM-SIP | **95 h** (CMFAS Academy) — most technical | 4–6 weeks |
| RES 12B | **68 h** combined (48 h 1B + 34 h 2B if separate) | 3–6 weeks |

Average ≈ **70–90 hours per exam → ~4–5 weeks part-time** (compresses to ~2–3 weeks per paper for a finance-background candidate). All three sequentially ≈ **~240 h ≈ 10–12 weeks**.

### Dates and scheduling

- **Rolling availability** — slots open continuously on the IBF Portal (weekday + some weekend sessions). Book as soon as your account is activated. This applies equally to **CM-SIP**.
- **Indicative nearest slots** (observed Oct 2026 via CMFAS Academy; changes constantly): **RES 12B ~13 Oct 2026**, **FMRP ~5 Nov 2026**. No indicative next-available slot was published for **CM-SIP** — check the portal directly.
- **Guidance:** book **4–6 weeks out** to create a study deadline.
- **Rescheduling:** up to **12pm one business day before**; **$81.75 incl. GST** admin fee; subject to availability; cannot change module or transfer to another candidate.
- **Study guide PDF:** available in-portal after registration; access **expires on exam day**.
- **Retakes:** no waiting period — rebook immediately (full fee applies).
- **Results:** shown on screen immediately; printable result slip from portal next business day.

Also: **create a personal IBF Portal account (free)** now — it keeps your records independent of employer changes, but don't book/ pay through it if the firm will register you corporately.

#### Booking rules and exam-day logistics

- **Book 4–6 weeks out** — book *before* you feel ready; the fixed deadline drives study (ExamPrep.sg's explicit advice).
- **Reschedule** up to **12pm one business day before** — **$81.75 incl. GST**, subject to availability; can't change module or transfer to another candidate.
- **Study-guide PDF access expires on exam day** — download/print it the day you register.
- **Retake** immediately if you fail (no waiting period), but **full fee** applies — so aim to pass first time.
- **Pass marks:** FMRP & RES = **75%**; CM-SIP = **70%**. No margin for guessing on the conduct papers.
- **Exam day:** bring original **NRIC/Passport/Employment Pass**; allowed a **non-programmable calculator** (essential for CM-SIP, rarely needed for FMRP/RES); arrive ~30 min early; phones/smartwatches/notes not allowed.
- **Results:** shown on screen immediately; printable slip from the portal next business day.

### Key exams to trade (Leonteq Trading Asia)

To be licensed to deal / take orders, MAS requires the representative to pass the **activity module(s)** for the licence activity, plus product modules where relevant. **Compliance decides the exact set — confirm first; it drives everything.**

Leonteq Asia = equity **structured investment solutions**, non-exchange (OTC) market maker. Working from the role ("order taking and execution", structured products, exotics):

| Priority | Exam | Why | Exemptible? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 — the gate** | **RES 12B** (= RES 1B + 2B) | The "Rules, Ethics and Skills" module for dealing in **securities & derivatives as a non-exchange member** — the licence exam for order taking/execution | **No** |
| **2** | **CM-SIP** | Product knowledge for **Specified Investment Products** (derivatives & structured products) — the products the desk trades | Possibly (MAS Notice SFA 04-N22) |
| 3 — only if applicable | FMRP | Only if dealing in **wholesale OTC FX / money market / rates** | **No** |
| — | CACS Paper 1 / 2 | Private banking / wealth advisory only — **not relevant** | — |

- If Leonteq's SG entity is an **SGX member**, the dealing module is **RES 1A + RES 2A** instead of RES 1B + 2B — confirm which.
- **RES 12B is the hard gate** (no exemption, 75% pass) — it is the one that unblocks dealing fastest.
- **CM-SIP** is the second key one; a quantitative degree exempts it **only** if it is specifically finance / financial engineering / computational finance — a statistics degree likely does **not** qualify, so assume you will sit it.
- **FMRP is probably not required** for an equity structured-products desk — it targets FX / rates / money-market dealers.

**Fastest route to trading:** **RES 12B + CM-SIP** (~6–8 weeks combined). RES alone clears the licence activity; CM-SIP covers the products. FMRP only if compliance confirms FX/rates are in scope.

### Content overlap analysis

**FMRP ↔ RES 12B — HIGH overlap (~50–60%).** Both are MAS conduct/regulatory papers drawn from overlapping source material:

| Theme | FMRP chapter | RES 1B / 2B chapter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Market structure, participants, regulators | 1. Introduction | The Capital Markets Industry in Singapore and Participants |
| Ethics & professional standards | 2. Ethics, Behavioural Standards and Professional Conduct | Ethics, Codes and Standards of Professional Conduct (Securities / Derivatives) |
| Market conduct & misconduct | 2 / 5. Execution and Handling of Orders | Market Conduct |
| Licensing / regulated activity | 1 (framework) | Licensing and Business Operations |
| AML / financial crime | 4. Governance, Risk Management and Compliance | Prevention of Financial Crimes |
| Dealing practices incl. OTC derivatives | 5. Execution and Handling of Orders | Over-the-Counter Derivatives + Derivatives Dealing Practices and Skills |

- **FMRP unique:** Confidentiality & Information Sharing (3), Confirmation & Settlement (6), Handling Market Disruptions (7), Benchmark Rate Setting (8 — Blue Book / FX-fixing specific).
- **RES unique:** CPFIS and Securities Dealing Practices (RES 1B, securities side) + the combined-paper structure.
- **RES 1B ↔ RES 2B overlap ~70% internally** (identical industry/licensing/market-conduct/ethics/financial-crime chapters; only the product-practice chapter differs). Sitting 1B first makes 2B significantly easier — and vice versa.

**CM-SIP — the outlier (~10–20% overlap).** It is product/technical knowledge of derivatives and structured products (options pricing, Greeks, payoffs, CFDs, barrier/binary options, structured notes/funds, 6 case studies = 22.5%), not conduct.

| Overlap with CM-SIP | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| CM-SIP 2 (Futures, Forwards & Swaps) & 4 (Options) ↔ RES 2B OTC Derivatives | Terminology/concepts only — RES tests conduct, CM-SIP tests valuation & mechanics |
| CM-SIP 11 (Investment Risks) ↔ FMRP 4 (risk mgmt) & RES dealing practices | Risk *awareness* overlaps; CM-SIP needs product-specific detail |
| CM-SIP has **no ethics / market-conduct / AML content** | Nothing shared with the conduct core |

### Can they be taken back-to-back?

#### Content-wise

- **Yes — RES 12B + FMRP, genuinely back-to-back.** Study the shared conduct core once, then add only FMRP's four unique chapters and RES's CPFIS/securities chapters. Comfortably sat within the same week, even consecutive days.
- **Caution — CM-SIP: no, not bundled with the others.** Its ~95 h of technical prep shares little with the conduct papers, so pairing saves no study time but adds fatigue right when precision matters most.
- **Note — RES 12B internally:** prefer sitting **RES 1B and RES 2B separately** — with the combined 12B, failing any part fails the whole paper and forces full-fee retakes.

#### Logistically

- Same portal, same venue, same fee model → a batch booking is straightforward.
- IBF books **per exam**; back-to-back means separate slots (you cannot sit two at once). Same-day AM/PM is possible **subject to availability**, but FMRP (2 h) + RES (1.5 h) = 3.5 h of testing in one day is draining, and both require **75%**.
- **Study-guide access expires on each exam's own day** — sequence materials so you do not lose the second exam's guide.
- **Rescheduling is per exam** — two back-to-back sittings = two independent $81.75 fees if plans change.
- If the firm registers you, it may book a batch to its own deadline.

#### Recommended sequence

1. **RES 12B (or 1B + 2B) + FMRP — back-to-back**, sharing one conduct study block.
2. **Gap of 4–6 weeks.**
3. **CM-SIP** as a standalone technical block.

**Tight single push (all conduct first):** RES 1B → RES 2B → FMRP within ~2–3 weeks, then CM-SIP after a gap.
