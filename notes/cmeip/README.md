
The CM-EIP Exam (previously Module 6) applies to all persons who wish to advise on securities, collective investment schemes and foreign exchange.

Exam Syllabus

- Introduction to Investing, Investments and Financial Markets
- Risk, Return and Time Calculations
- Key Drivers of Market Movements and Asset Values
- Foreign Exchange
- Company Analysis and Understanding Financial Statements
- Equity Securities
- Deposits and Fixed-Income Securities
- Portfolio Management
- ETFs, Unit Trusts, REITS, and Insurance
- Warrants
- Technical Analysis and Quantitative Analysis
- Case Studies

About the Exam

- 90 MCQs (computer-based)
- Duration: 2.5 hours
- Passing score: 70%
- Fee: $190 (Members) or $230 (NM)
