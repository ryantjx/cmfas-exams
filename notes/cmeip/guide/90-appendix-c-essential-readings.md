# Appendix C — Essential Readings

The source list runs to **seven items**. Candidates should complete the essential readings before attempting the examination. Use the current edition of each; page references in older editions will not match.

## How to read the list efficiently

- **Items 1–5 overlap heavily.** They are general investments texts covering risk and return, asset classes, valuation and portfolio theory. Read **one** thoroughly — Bodie, Kane and Marcus (item 3) is the most compact for the risk-return and CAPM material, and Reilly and Brown (item 1) is the most comprehensive on asset classes. Use the others as reference for a topic the first does not explain well.
- **Item 5** is the CFA Institute's portfolio and equity analysis text and maps most closely onto Chapters 6 and 8 of this syllabus — equity valuation, relative valuation ratios and portfolio management.
- **Item 6** is the only technical analysis text and covers Chapter 11. Read it for the chart patterns and indicators; the examination tests recognition and interpretation, not construction.
- **Item 7** is the reference work on the portfolio management process, investment policy statements and performance measurement — Chapters 8 and 9.
- **Nothing on the list covers Chapters 1, 4, 7, 10 or 12 in depth.** For those, the official study guide and this teaching guide are the primary sources: Chapter 1 (investor classification and market structure), Chapter 4 (FX quotations and forwards), Chapter 7 (fixed income, duration and convertibles), Chapter 10 (warrants) and Chapter 12 (case studies) are syllabus-specific and not covered by general investment texts at this level of detail.
- **Singapore-specific content** — the SFA investor definitions, the Deposit Insurance Scheme, SGS, the CPF Investment Scheme, the Code on CIS, REIT rules and the Business Trusts Act — is not in any of these books. Use the MAS and SGX publications referenced in the chapters.

## Exam cues

- **Seven items** on the list, across **five publishers**.
- **Items 1–5** are general investments texts; **item 6** is technical analysis; **item 7** is portfolio management.
- **Bodie, Kane and Marcus** is the most compact for risk-return and CAPM; **Reilly and Brown** is the most comprehensive on asset classes.
- **McMillan et al.** maps most closely to the equity valuation and portfolio chapters.
- **Maginn et al.** is the reference for the IPS and performance measurement.
- **Kahn** is the only technical analysis text.
- **No listed text covers** investor classification, FX mechanics, fixed income duration and convertibles, warrants, or the case studies at syllabus depth — use the official guide and this teaching guide.
- **Singapore regulatory content is not in any listed text.** Use MAS and SGX sources.
- **Trap:** the list is a reading list, not a syllabus. Reading all seven does not substitute for working the calculations in Chapters 2, 6, 7, 8 and 10.
