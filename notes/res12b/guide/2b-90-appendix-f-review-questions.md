# Appendix F — Review Questions

The Part II review questions are for **familiarisation with the scope and format** of the examination. They are **not preparatory study material** and **do not cover all the material tested**. The correct answer for each question is shown in **bold** in the source booklet.

## How to use them

1. Read the relevant chapter first; the questions test recognition, not first-time learning.
2. Answer before checking, and write down the rule you relied on.
3. Where an answer turns on a distinction — agent versus principal, listed versus unlisted SIP, CAR versus CKA — state the distinction in your own words.
4. Return to the chapter for any question you miss; the explanation in the chapter is the study material, not the answer key.

## Scope of the Part II questions

The source questions cover: **licensing changes** and notification duties; **cessation of regulated activity**; the **five-year** record-retention requirement; **hunting for stops** and other order-handling misconduct; the forms of **market misconduct** under the SFA; **front running**; **OTC derivative** products and the regulatory regime; the **ethical framework** and its resolution process; **back-tested performance** presentation; **best execution**; **CKA exemptions**; **regulatory risk**; the principal **AML statute**; action on a **sanctions list** hit; and the point that **terrorism financing may involve legitimate funds and need not involve large amounts**.

## Exam cues

- **The questions sample, they do not cover.** A clean run through them is not evidence of readiness.
- **Learn the distinction behind each question,** not the letter.
- **Trap:** several questions turn on the **five-year** retention rule and on **which notice** applies (SFA04-N02 for AML/CFT), so those two facts recur and are worth memorising exactly.
