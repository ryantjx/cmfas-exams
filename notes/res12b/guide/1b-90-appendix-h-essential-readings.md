# Appendix H — Essential readings

The source list runs to **twenty items**, mixing primary legislation on **Singapore Statutes Online** (`https://sso.agc.gov.sg`, formerly `https://statutes.agc.gov.sg`) with MAS notices, guidelines and circulars on `https://www.mas.gov.sg`, plus CPF material on `https://www.cpf.gov.sg`. The full list follows, grouped by the part of the syllabus it supports.

Item 2 governs business conduct — statements of account, trust accounts, confidentiality, conflicts, records. Item 3 carries the **base capital** and **financial and margin** requirements that underpin Chapters 2 and 5.

## How to read the list efficiently

**Read the notice, not the Act, for operational detail.** The SFA creates the offences, but the *conduct* requirements a representative applies day to day — statements, trust accounts, records — are in the Licensing and Conduct of Business Regulations (item 2) and the notices (items 6, 7, 9, 16).

**Read the guideline when the question is about discretion.** Fit and proper (item 5) and licence criteria (item 4) are judgement-based tests, so the guideline's factors matter more than any single rule.

**Read across jurisdictions for financial crime.** The CDSA, MACMA and TSOFA (items 13–15) operate as a package with the AML notice (item 16); a question about suspicious transactions usually needs at least two of them.

**Do not neglect the tax and data statutes.** Items 11, 19 and 20 appear peripheral but support questions on confidentiality, tax-crime indicators and the handling of client information.

## Exam cues

- **Item 5 (FSG-01) is the fit-and-proper source**; **item 4 (SFA 04-G01) is the licensing-criteria source.** Confusing the two is a common error.
- **Item 9 (SFA 04-N12) contains paragraph 29D**, the overseas-listed product Risk Warning Statement requirement examined through Appendix D.
- **Item 16 (SFA04-N02) is the CMI AML/CFT notice**; **item 18 (Notice 626) is the bank equivalent**, included for comparison rather than direct application.
- **Item 3 carries the financial and margin requirements**, including base capital — not item 2.
- **Item 12 is CPF-published, not MAS-published**, which is worth remembering when a question turns on the status of CPFIS guidance.
