# Final revision checklist

Before an examination, be able to explain the difference between:

- primary and secondary markets;
- exchange and OTC trading;
- MAS, SGX/SGX RegCo, trading venues, clearing houses and CDP;
- AI, II, EI and retail customers;
- CAR and CKA;
- EIP and SIP;
- customer money and customer assets;
- trust and custody accounts;
- false trading, manipulation, deceptive devices, bucketing, front-running, churning and pre-arranged trades;
- inside information and generally available information;
- agent and principal execution;
- price, cost, speed, likelihood, size and execution quality in best execution;
- placement, layering and integration;
- CDD, simplified CDD, EDD, PEP controls and ongoing monitoring;
- STR filing and tipping-off; and
- operational, market, credit, liquidity, technology, regulatory, interconnected and AML/CFT risks.
