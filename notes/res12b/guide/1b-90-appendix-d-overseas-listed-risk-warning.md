# Appendix D — Overseas-listed risk warning

The warning covers different regulation/investor protection, legal systems and redress, reporting/accounting language, repatriation and foreign-investor restrictions, tax, currency/exchange controls, additional fees, foreign counterparties/correspondent brokers and political/economic/social conditions. Obtain acknowledgement before first transaction.
