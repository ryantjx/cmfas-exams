# Appendix E — Excluded Investment Products

The list includes ordinary shares, certain share units, issuer rights/options/derivatives, business-trust units and derivatives, listed real-estate CIS units, certain tightly limited CIS structures, debentures other than specified asset-backed securities/structured notes, immediate currency-exchange arrangements and stapled combinations of EIPs. Classification must remain accurate and current.
