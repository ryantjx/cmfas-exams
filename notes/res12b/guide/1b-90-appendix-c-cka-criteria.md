# Appendix C — CKA criteria

The education and professional-qualification routes are similar. Experience is product-specific: at least six CIS/ILP transactions for those products, or six other unlisted-SIP transactions for other unlisted SIPs in the preceding three years, or three consecutive years’ relevant work in the preceding ten years. An approved independent module may help.
