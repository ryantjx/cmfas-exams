# CPF Investment Scheme (CPFIS)

Using CPF savings to invest. The examination tests the OA/SA distinction, the investment account, the thresholds and limits, and what happens at 55, on bankruptcy and on death.

## Learning Objectives

The candidate should be able to:

- Distinguish CPFIS-OA from CPFIS-SA and their permitted products.
- Explain the account-opening and operating process, including the agent bank's role.
- Apply the investible-balance and instrument limits.
- State how dividends, losses and charges are treated.
- Explain release at 55, and the treatment of bankruptcy and death.

## 6.1 General Information and History

The Government allowed CPF members to invest **Ordinary Account (OA)** savings in approved products from **1987**. The scheme was broadened in **January 2001** so that **Special Account (SA)** savings could also be invested, creating the two-part structure that exists today.

| Part | Source of funds | Investment account required |
|---|---|---|
| **CPFIS-OA** | Ordinary Account savings only | **Yes** — a CPF Investment Account with an agent bank |
| **CPFIS-SA** | Special Account savings only | **No** |

The two parts permit **different products**. The scheme exists to let members who accept investment risk seek a higher return than the CPF interest rate; a member must weigh objectives, time horizon and risk appetite, and compare the expected return against the interest the money would otherwise earn in the CPF account.

## 6.2 Differences between CPFIS-OA and CPFIS-SA

### 6.2.1 Types of Investments Allowed and Investment Limits

| CPFIS-OA | CPFIS-SA |
|---|---|
| Fixed deposits | Fixed deposits |
| Singapore Government Bonds | Singapore Government Bonds |
| Singapore Government Treasury Bills | Singapore Government Treasury Bills |
| Statutory board bonds | Statutory board bonds (**secondary market only**) |
| Bonds guaranteed by the Singapore Government | Bonds guaranteed by the Singapore Government |
| Annuities | Annuities |
| Endowment insurance policies | Endowment insurance policies |
| Fund management accounts | Selected ILPs |
| Investment-linked insurance products (ILPs) | Selected unit trusts |
| Unit trusts | Selected ETFs |
| ETFs | — |

**Up to 35%** of investable savings may be invested in **shares, property funds (REITs) and corporate bonds** under CPFIS-OA. **Up to 10%** may be invested in **gold** — gold ETFs and other gold products.

### 6.2.2 Investible Balance

The first **S$60,000** of a member's **combined** CPF accounts earns extra interest, capped at **S$20,000** from the Ordinary Account. To preserve that extra interest, only money **above S$20,000 in the OA** and **above S$40,000 in the SA** is investible.

A member may continue to service **regular-premium insurance policies** and **agent-bank fees** from CPF savings even when the OA balance falls below S$20,000. **Recurring single-premium policies** and **regular savings plans for unit trusts** may not be serviced this way.

## 6.3 What is a CPF Investment Account?

A CPF Investment Account is opened with a **CPFIS agent bank** to settle purchases and sales and to record the member's holdings and transactions. It is required for CPFIS-OA and not for CPFIS-SA.

## 6.4 Eligibility Criteria

The member must be **18 or older**, must **not be an undischarged bankrupt**, and must hold investible balances above the OA and SA thresholds in 6.2.2. From **1 October 2018**, every new CPFIS investor must complete the **Self-Awareness Questionnaire (SAQ)** before investing, which tests basic financial knowledge and whether CPFIS suits the member.

## 6.5 Opening a CPFIS Investment Account

A member approaches **DBS, OCBC or UOB** — the three CPFIS agent banks — online or at a branch, bringing an **identity card**, the **SAQ status document** and any **CPF statement** so the bank can verify the CPF account number. A member may hold **only one** CPF Investment Account at any time.

## 6.6 Processes for Purchase and Sale (CPFIS-OA only)

After opening the account, the member must give the dealer the account details **before** transacting. The agent bank settles with the CPF Board and the product providers and maintains the holdings record.

A member may sell SGX-ST-listed investments **one trading day after** the purchase date where the agent bank has accepted the purchase as a CPFIS-OA trade. The bank accepts only if the broker keyed the trade **on the day the contract was made**, the trade succeeded, and the member has **sufficient investible funds**, including the stock and gold limits, to settle.

## 6.7 Service Providers under the CPFIS

| Instrument | Permitted provider |
|---|---|
| Fixed deposits | CPFIS-included fixed deposit banks |
| Singapore Government Bonds | Bond dealers or brokers |
| Singapore Government Treasury Bills | Bond dealers |
| Statutory board bonds | Bond dealers or brokers |
| Government-guaranteed bonds | Brokers |
| Annuities, endowments, ILPs | CPFIS-included insurance companies |
| Unit trusts | Fund management companies, investment administrators |
| Fund management accounts | Fund management companies |
| Shares, property funds, corporate bonds, ETFs | Brokers |
| Gold ETFs | Brokers |
| Other gold products | Sold through the agent bank; bought through the bank offering the product, which requires that bank's investment account |

## 6.8 Criteria for Inclusion of Investment Products

Most instruments are included only on the **product provider's application** and after **CPF Board review**; unit trusts, ILPs and ETFs must also be evaluated by the CPF Board's appointed **investment consultant**. The exceptions needing no application are gold products offered by CPF agent banks, Singapore Government Bonds and Treasury Bills.

All CPFIS investment must be in **Singapore dollars** unless stated otherwise, and CPFIS investments **cannot be assigned, pledged or used as collateral**.

| Instrument | Inclusion criteria |
|---|---|
| **Fixed deposits** | Offered by a CPFIS-included bank that is locally incorporated with minimum capital funds of **S$1.5 billion** and a good credit rating; a qualifying subsidiary of such a bank; or a foreign bank with **Qualifying Full Bank** privileges |
| **Government bonds and T-bills** | Traded in Singapore dollars and scripless |
| **Statutory board bonds** | Issued by a statutory board, listed on the **SGX-ST Main Board**, traded in SGD, not offered only to institutional or accredited investors under **sections 274 or 275 SFA**, not subject to secondary-market trading restrictions, and issued with an SFA-compliant prospectus or an information memorandum |
| **Government-guaranteed bonds** | Listed on the **SGX-ST Main Board** and traded in SGD |
| **Annuities, endowments, ILPs** | Offered by a CPFIS-included insurer; the **life insured must be the member**; only **single-premium or recurring single-premium** policies — new regular-premium policies excluded from **1 January 2001**; endowment maturity no later than the member's **62nd birthday** or the prescribed minimum retirement age, whichever is later |
| **Unit trusts** | Managed by a CPFIS-included fund manager; evaluated as within the **top 25th percentile** of the global peer group; **TER** within CPF Board caps; **no sales charge**; preferably a track record of at least **3 years**; compliant with the CPF Investment Guidelines |
| **ETFs** | Listed on SGX; CPFIS-included fund manager; efficient index tracking with low tracking error; TER considered; **no sales charge**; **physical replication** (full or partial); preferably at least **3 years** of tracking record; compliant with the CPF Investment Guidelines |
| **Gold ETFs** | Listed on SGX-ST; backed by physical gold meeting the **London Good Delivery Rules**; offered by a Singapore-incorporated company, or through a local representative for a MAS-recognised foreign ETF; the company must let the agent bank appoint CPF unitholders as proxies |

## 6.9 Transfer of Monies to the CPF Ordinary Account

A member may transfer the investment account's cash balance to the OA at any time through the agent bank's ATM, phone or internet banking, or over the counter. The bank transfers the balance **automatically at month end** where the account has been inactive — no investment transactions — for **two consecutive months**, and returns unused CPF from an unsuccessful **IPO** application at month end.

## 6.10 Dividends and Profits Earned

Dividends and profits from CPFIS-OA and CPFIS-SA investments **cannot be withdrawn as cash**, because the purpose is to grow retirement savings. They may be used for other CPF schemes subject to those schemes' terms. **Losses do not need to be made good.**

## 6.11 Charges

Charges may be paid from CPF savings and may be levied by product or service providers and by the agent bank for the account and for settlement and accounting. The caps were reduced in two phases:

| Phase | Sales charge cap | Wrap fee cap |
|---|---|---|
| Before October 2018 | 3% | 1% |
| **October 2018** | 1.5% | 0.7% |
| **October 2020** | **Removed** | **0.4%** |

## 6.12 Release of Investment Holdings

At **55**, a member may apply to the CPF Board to withdraw CPFIS-OA and CPFIS-SA investments and the investment-account cash balance, provided the **Full Retirement Sum (FRS)** has been set aside — or the **Basic Retirement Sum (BRS)** with sufficient property charged or pledged in the Retirement Account.

- **CPFIS-OA:** the Board tells the agent bank to close the account; the member withdraws investments and cash after notification, the investments are transferred into the member's own name, and the member may liquidate and receive the proceeds directly.
- **CPFIS-SA:** the Board tells the product providers to transfer the investments into the member's name, after which the member may liquidate and receive the proceeds.

If the FRS or BRS is not set aside, the investments are **not** transferred: liquidation proceeds are credited to the CPF Investment Account (OA) or to the Special Account.

## 6.13 Bankruptcy

An **undischarged bankrupt** may hold or liquidate existing CPFIS investments, with proceeds credited back to the CPF Investment Account or Special Account, but **may not make new investments** using CPF money. At 55 the FRS or BRS requirement still applies before withdrawal. CPFIS investments and investment-account cash are **protected from creditors and the Official Assignee** while they remain within the scheme.

## 6.14 Death

CPFIS investments and investment-account cash are **not covered by CPF nomination**; they form part of the **estate**. The administrator or executor must produce **Letters of Administration** or a **Grant of Probate** to claim them from the agent bank or product providers. CPFIS insurance policies with a **revocable** nomination made with the insurer bypass that process and pay the nominated beneficiaries directly; without a nomination the proceeds are distributed with the rest of the estate. **Irrevocable** nomination is not allowed for CPFIS insurance policies. On death the CPFIS assets **cease to be protected** and may satisfy the deceased's creditors under the Probate and Administration Act.

## Exam cues

- **The account is the dividing line:** CPFIS-OA needs a CPF Investment Account; CPFIS-SA does not. One account only, at DBS, OCBC or UOB.
- **Two limits, two numbers:** **35%** of investable savings in shares, property funds and corporate bonds; **10%** in gold.
- **Investible balance:** only OA above **S$20,000** and SA above **S$40,000**, preserving extra interest on the first **S$60,000** combined.
- **Charges moved twice:** October 2018 cut sales charges 3%→1.5% and wrap fees 1%→0.7%; October 2020 removed sales charges and cut the wrap-fee cap to **0.4%**.
- **55 needs the retirement sum:** FRS, or BRS with sufficient property charge or pledge — otherwise proceeds return to the CPF accounts.
- **Death is the trap:** CPFIS is **outside** CPF nomination and inside the estate; only revocable insurer nominations bypass probate, and protection from creditors ends at death.
