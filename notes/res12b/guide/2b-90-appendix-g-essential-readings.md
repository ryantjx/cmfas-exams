# Appendix G — Essential Readings

The Part I list of twenty instruments is reproduced in **[Appendix H of Part I](../chapter-1b-appendix-h-essential-readings/)**. This page adds the Part II sources and states how to read the list.

## Part II additions

| Instrument | Why it matters on a derivatives book |
|---|---|
| **Securities and Futures Act** | Statutory basis for licensing, market conduct and the OTC derivatives regime; Part II's Chapter 3 and Chapter 4 turn on it |
| **Securities and Futures (Licensing and Conduct of Business) Regulations** | Business conduct, customer assets, records and representative notification |
| **Securities and Futures (Financial and Margin Requirements) Regulations** | Capital and margin requirements that govern the firm's own derivatives positions |
| **Securities and Futures (Reporting of Derivatives Contracts) Regulations** | The reporting obligation for OTC derivatives contracts to a trade repository |
| **MAS Notice SFA04-N02** (AML/CFT) | Customer due diligence, enhanced measures, screening and STR obligations |
| **MAS Guidelines to SFA04-N02, Appendix B** | The suspicious-transaction examples reproduced as this part's Appendix E |
| **MAS Notice SFA04-N12** (Sale of Investment Products) | SIP classification and the CAR, CKA and risk-warning requirements |
| **MAS Notice SFA04-N22** (Competency Requirements) | Examination and exemption basis for representatives |
| **MAS Guidelines on Fit and Proper Criteria (FSG-G01)** | The fit-and-proper test applied to representatives |
| **SGX-DT rules and directives** | Exchange-traded derivatives: contract specifications, margining, position limits |
| **SGX-DC clearing rules** | Clearing of exchange-traded and eligible OTC derivatives |
| **ICE Clear Singapore, APEX and APEX Clear rules** | Alternative clearing venues for listed and OTC contracts |
| **ISDA Master Agreement and credit support documents** | The contractual architecture of the OTC derivatives relationship |
| **The Singapore Guide to Conduct and Market Practices for the Wholesale Financial Markets** | The market-conduct standard for FX, NDF, money-market and derivative dealing |
| **FX Global Code and IMAS Code of Ethics** | Execution principles and the ethical standards incorporated into the dealing chapters |

## How to read the list efficiently

- **Statute first, notice second.** Every notice is made under the SFA; knowing the statutory power makes the notice's purpose obvious.
- **Read notices as pairs.** SFA04-N02 (AML/CFT) governs the client file; SFA04-N12 (sale of investment products) governs the suitability gate; SFA04-N22 governs who may advise.
- **Rules are venue-specific.** SGX-DT, SGX-DC, ICE Clear Singapore and APEX each have their own rulebook; the exam tests which venue and which rulebook, not the text of a rule.
- **The market-practice guides are conduct, not law.** The Singapore Guide and the FX Global Code state best practice; breaching them is evidence of poor conduct but is not itself an offence.

## Exam cues

- **The SFA underpins everything;** the notices are where the operational requirements live.
- **Three notices, three jobs:** N02 = AML/CFT, N12 = SIP sale and suitability, N22 = competency.
- **Trap:** rules are venue-specific — an SGX-DT contract specification question is not answered by the SGX-DC clearing rules.
