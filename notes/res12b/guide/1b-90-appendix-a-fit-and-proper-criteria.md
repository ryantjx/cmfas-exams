# Appendix A — Guidelines on Fit and Proper Criteria

MAS assesses whether a person is fit and proper on three factors, and the guideline lists the specific matters that may indicate a failure on each. The examination tests the **three factors** and the **range of matters considered**, not the wording.

## The three factors

| Factor | What it asks |
|---|---|
| **Honesty, integrity and reputation** | Is the person's conduct and record consistent with someone who can be trusted with other people's money and with market integrity? |
| **Competence and capability** | Does the person have the qualifications, skills, experience and soundness of judgement for the role? |
| **Financial soundness** | Is the person's financial position such that personal pressure does not impair judgement? |

Failure on one factor is **not automatically determinative**. The assessment weighs the **seriousness** of the matter, its **relevance** to the duties to be performed, and the **time** that has elapsed, together with any remediation and the person's candour.

## Matters that may indicate a failure

The guideline sets out an extensive list. Read it as categories rather than a list to memorise.

### Regulatory and enforcement history

- **i.** Has had any **civil penalty enforcement action** taken against it or him by MAS or by any other regulatory authority, under any law in any jurisdiction.
- **ii.** Has **contravened, or abetted another person in breach of**, any laws or regulations, business rules or codes of conduct, whether in Singapore or elsewhere.
- **iii.** Has been the subject of any **investigation or disciplinary proceeding**, or been issued a **warning or reprimand**, by MAS, any other regulatory authority, an operator of a market, trade repository or clearing facility, any professional body or government agency, whether in Singapore or elsewhere.

### Integrity and honesty

- **iv.** Has been **refused a fidelity or surety bond**, whether in Singapore or elsewhere.
- **v.** Has demonstrated an **unwillingness to comply** with any regulatory requirement or to uphold any professional and ethical standards, whether in Singapore or elsewhere.
- **vi.** Has been **untruthful**, or provided **false or misleading information**, to MAS, or has been **uncooperative** in any dealings with MAS or any other regulatory authority in any jurisdiction.

### Additional matters where the person is an individual

Where the relevant person is an individual, the guideline adds that the person:

- **i.** is or has been a **director, partner, substantial shareholder or concerned in the management** of a business that has been **censured, disciplined, prosecuted or convicted** of a criminal offence, or been the subject of any disciplinary or criminal investigation or proceeding, in Singapore or elsewhere, in relation to any matter that took place while the person held that role;
- **ii.** is or has been a director, partner, substantial shareholder or concerned in the management of a business that has been **suspended or refused membership or registration** by MAS, any other regulatory authority, an operator of a market, trade repository or clearing facility, any professional body or government agency;
- **iii.** has been a director, partner, substantial shareholder or concerned in the management of a business that has gone into **insolvency, liquidation or administration** during the period when, or **within one year after**, the person held that role;
- **iv.** has been **dismissed or asked to resign** from office, employment, a position of trust, or a fiduciary or similar appointment;
- **v.** is or has been **subject to disciplinary proceedings by a current or former employer**;
- **vi.** has been **disqualified from acting as a director** or in any managerial capacity; and
- **vii.** has been an officer **found liable for an offence committed by a body corporate**, where the offence was proved to have been committed with the officer's **consent or connivance**, or was attributable to the officer's **neglect**.

## How the assessment is applied

Three points follow from the structure.

**The list is not exhaustive and not automatic.** A single matter does not disqualify a person; MAS considers its seriousness, its relevance to the duties and how long ago it occurred. The word "may" in every limb is deliberate.

**Candour matters as much as the underlying conduct.** Items (vi) and the final list penalise untruthfulness and uncooperativeness in dealings with the regulator, which means a disclosed and remediated failure can be treated more favourably than a concealed one.

**The scope reaches the businesses a person ran.** The final list extends the assessment beyond the individual's own conduct to businesses they directed or managed. That is why a representative must disclose directorships and management roles, not only personal disciplinary history.

## Exam cues

- **Three factors:** honesty, integrity and reputation; competence and capability; financial soundness.
- **Not automatic.** Consider the seriousness, relevance to the duties, and time elapsed. A single failure does not automatically disqualify.
- **Scope widens for individuals** to businesses they were a director, partner, substantial shareholder of or concerned in managing — including censure, refused registration, insolvency within one year, disqualification and corporate offences attributable to consent, connivance or neglect.
- **Candour is assessed.** Untruthfulness or uncooperativeness with MAS is itself a listed matter.
- **Trap:** an old, disclosed and remediated matter is not the same as a recent, concealed one, and the guideline treats them differently.
