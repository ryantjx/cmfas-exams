# Appendix G — Review questions

Candidates should note that the **sole purpose of the Review Questions is to familiarise candidates with the scope and general nature of the examinations, and with the format of the examination questions**. They are **not intended as preparatory study material**, and **do not cover all the material tested**. The correct answer for each question is shown in **bold** in the source booklet and is reproduced as **Answer** below.

## Chapter 2 — Licensing and Business Operations

**1.** Under the SFA, a CMS licence holder must inform MAS if there is a change in its records relating to:

- **a.** The name of the CMS licence under which the business is carried on.
- **b.** The back-office settlement infrastructure used by the CMS licence holder.
- **c.** The address of the principal place of business in respect of the licensed activity.
- **d.** The contact numbers of the CMS licence holder's client pool.

Options: (a) and (c) · (b) and (c) · (a) and (d) · (a) and (b)

**Answer: (a) and (c).**

**2.** To satisfy the fit-and-proper criteria for registration as a Representative, an individual must:

- **c.** Be of minimally satisfactory tertiary educational qualification in Singapore.

**Answer: a.**

**3.** What document must a CMS licence holder obtain from a retail investor before accepting any transactions in overseas-listed investment products?

**Answer: c.**

**5.** Under which circumstances may a CMS licence holder advance its own moneys to a customer's trust account?

**12.** Gaston, an appointed representative, manages large-cap equity portfolios for his clients. Recently, due to economic recession, the performance of large-cap equity markets has been lacklustre. ## How to use these questions

These questions **familiarise candidates with scope, general nature and format**; they do not cover all examination material and are not a substitute for the chapters. Several answers turn on a single distinguishing word:

- **Q1** turns on which record changes are notifiable — the licence **name** and the **address of the principal place of business**, not back-office infrastructure or client contact numbers.
- **Q3** turns on the **signed acknowledgement of the Risk Warning Statement** — verbal explanation, whether alone or as a mailed statement, is insufficient.
- **Q6** tests the **extra-territorial reach** of the SFA: the location of execution and the currency of payment are irrelevant.
- **Q9** and **Q10** test the boundary of a list: collateral need not be 100%, and "restraint sanctions" is not a category of consequence for market misconduct.
- **Q17** turns on the **designated market maker** designation plus net assets, which places the entity in the institutional category rather than the accredited one.
- **Q21** is the one product among the four that falls under the **35%** CPFIS-OA sub-limit — corporate bonds, not fixed deposits, Government bonds or endowment policies.
- **Q23** turns on the **CPF balance threshold**, not on employment status or age.
- **Q26** distinguishes **layering** (reselling and dispersing proceeds through multiple accounts) from placement and integration.

The expanded question bank in `notes/res12b/RES12B_QUESTIONS.md` covers these themes and adds application and calculation questions beyond the orientation set.
