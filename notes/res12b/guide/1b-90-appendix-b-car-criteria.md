# Appendix B — Criteria for the Assessment of a Customer Account Review

The **Customer Account Review (CAR)** applies to **listed** Specified Investment Products. A customer who satisfies any one of four routes may be assessed as possessing the knowledge or experience for the listed SIP concerned.

## The four routes

### Route 1 — Academic qualification

The customer holds a **diploma or higher qualification** in one of the listed subjects:

- accountancy;
- actuarial science;
- business, business administration, business management or business studies;
- capital markets;
- commerce;
- economics;
- finance;
- financial engineering;
- financial planning;
- computational finance; and
- insurance.

The subject matter must be relevant. A degree in an unrelated discipline does not satisfy this route.

### Route 2 — Professional finance qualification

The customer holds a **professional finance-related qualification**, for example:

| Qualification | Awarding body |
|---|---|
| **Chartered Financial Analyst (CFA)** Examination | CFA Institute, USA |
| **ACCA** Qualifications | Association of Chartered Certified Accountants |
| **Associate Wealth Planner** or **Certified Financial Planner** | Certified Financial Planners Board of Standards, USA |
| **Certified Financial Risk Manager** Programme | Global Association of Risk Professionals, USA |
| **Chartered Alternative Investment Analyst** Examination | Chartered Alternative Investment Analyst Association, USA |
| **Chartered Financial Consultant** | American College, USA |

### Route 3 — Transaction experience in listed SIPs

The customer has transacted in **listed** Specified Investment Products, with two limbs depending on the product:

- **(a)** for transactions in **listed** SIPs, at least **six transactions in the preceding three years**; or
- **(b)** for the product category concerned, the equivalent transaction history the Notice specifies.

The three-year window is rolling, so transactions older than three years do not count.

### Route 4 — Relevant work experience

The customer has a minimum of **three consecutive years of working experience in the past ten years** in:

- the **development** of investment products;
- the **structuring** of investment products;
- the **management** of investment products;
- the **sale** of investment products;
- the **trading** of investment products;
- **research on and analysis** of investment products; or
- the **provision of training** in investment products.

Work experience in **accountancy, actuarial science, treasury or financial risk management** is also considered relevant.

The three years must be **consecutive**, within the last **ten years**, and the experience must have been substantive rather than incidental.

## What happens if the customer fails the CAR

Where a customer is assessed as **not** possessing the knowledge or experience to understand a listed SIP:

- the firm must **notify the customer in writing**;
- the firm must obtain the customer's **written confirmation** that they wish to proceed;
- the firm must **explain the general features and risks** of the product in writing; and
- the firm must state that the customer is **responsible for understanding the products**.

A negative CAR therefore **does not bar** a listed-SIP account. It converts the firm's obligation from assessment to documented warning and customer acknowledgement.

## Exam cues

- **CAR is for listed SIPs.** Unlisted SIPs take a **CKA**, which is a different test with a one-year validity.
- **Four routes:** academic qualification, professional finance qualification, **six transactions in the preceding three years**, or **three consecutive years in the past ten** doing product development, structuring, management, sale, trading, research or training.
- **Also relevant experience:** accountancy, actuarial science, treasury and financial risk management.
- **Candour of the test:** the qualification must be in a listed subject, the three years must be consecutive and within ten years, and the six transactions must fall inside the three-year window.
- **Negative CAR does not block the account.** The firm must notify in writing, obtain written confirmation, explain features and risks in writing, and state that the customer is responsible.
- **Trap:** a customer cannot satisfy the CAR by a qualification in an unrelated field, and a short period of relevant work does not aggregate to three consecutive years.
