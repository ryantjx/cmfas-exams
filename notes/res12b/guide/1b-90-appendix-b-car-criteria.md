# Appendix B — CAR criteria

Knowledge/experience may be demonstrated by prescribed diploma/degree subjects, professional finance qualifications, at least six listed-SIP transactions in the preceding three years, or three consecutive years of relevant investment-product work in the preceding ten years. An independent learning module may help where the customer initially fails.
